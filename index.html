Late one night I read a headline: “Anthropic worth more than India's top 10 companies combined.” A company founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers had raced past decades of Indian IT in about five years.

India has the people, the coders and the ambition. I couldn't stop asking the question, and by dawn I had my answer:

It isn't a talent problem. It's a direction problem.

Chapter 3: Direction first

The compass came centuries before the mechanical clock. People worked out where they were going long before they measured how fast.

We train 17-year-olds to run faster. Nobody hands them a compass. So I decided to build one.

Naman

Chapter 4: Building Thryve

Your work is the résumé. Thryve is the proof-of-work career network for India's 18–22-year-olds. Every club, project and gig becomes proof on one living profile, vouched for by the people you built with. Students join free; employers pay.

  • Explore & learnCareer paths, startups and industries, made practical.
  • Access opportunitiesInternships, projects and early work.
  • Build in communitiesMUNs, NGOs and student groups doing real work.
  • Get discoveredCompanies hire on proven skills.
